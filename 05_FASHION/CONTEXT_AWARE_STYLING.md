# Context-Aware Styling

Fashion decisions derive from:
- location
- weather
- season
- time of day
- activity
- cultural context
- narrative tone
- character preferences

## Styling rule

The outfit should look chosen by the character for the situation, not chosen only to decorate the image.

## Constraints

- practical footwear for walking
- weather-compatible layering
- culturally appropriate coverage
- color harmony with the location without camouflage
- accessories limited to useful or character-consistent items
