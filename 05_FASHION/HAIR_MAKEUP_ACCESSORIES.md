# Hair, Makeup, and Accessories

## Hair

Adapt arrangement, not identity. Consider humidity, wind, activity, clothing collars, and headwear.

## Makeup

Use natural, climate-aware makeup. Increase formality only when the setting supports it.

## Accessories

Select by function first:
- sunglasses for bright light
- umbrella for rain
- scarf for cold or wind
- practical bag for travel
- restrained jewelry for refinement

Avoid accessory overload.
