# Camera Decision Engine

Choose camera treatment from the story.

## Portrait emphasis
- moderate telephoto perspective
- shallow to moderate depth of field
- controlled background separation

## Environmental portrait
- normal to mild wide perspective
- enough depth to identify the location
- subject integrated into architecture or landscape

## Dynamic travel moment
- natural motion
- slightly imperfect framing allowed
- shutter behavior consistent with movement

Avoid extreme wide-angle distortion near the face unless intentionally requested.
