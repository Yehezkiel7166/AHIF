# Operational Resilience Contract Test

## Assertions

1. OR0–OR9 stages exist and are ordered.
2. Empty registries resolve to `not-evaluated`.
3. Approval is distinct from execution and independent validation.
4. Missing evidence cannot create a passing exercise.
5. RTO and RPO remain declarations unless measured.
6. Every runbook has stop and abort conditions.
7. Exercise type cannot be upgraded by interpretation.
8. Event records are append-only and digest-linked.
9. Identity continuity checks are mandatory for recovery.
10. Recovery cannot silently mutate adapter support tiers.
