# Recovery Regression

## Assertions

- R1 duplicate removal does not alter material meaning.
- R2 lighting repair does not alter activity or identity directives.
- R3 compiler-plan repair preserves accepted reasoning.
- R4 rollback removes unsupported material facts before recompilation.
- R5 decision rollback reruns affected reasoning dependencies.
- R6 rejection emits no release-eligible final prompt.

## Loop protection

The same finding must not trigger the same ineffective repair repeatedly. After one failed identical repair, escalate the recovery level or reject.
