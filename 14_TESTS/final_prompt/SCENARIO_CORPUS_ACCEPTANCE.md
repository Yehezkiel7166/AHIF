# Scenario Corpus Acceptance

A release-level scenario passes when:

- the compact request can be normalized without hidden required input;
- visual decisions are causally supported;
- identity invariants remain dominant;
- the compiler output is complete and contradiction-free;
- QA produces stable codes and eligibility;
- the explainable summary reports decisions and confidence without private reasoning;
- the final package conforms to the release schema.

The v1.6 corpus contains Kyoto cold morning and Tokyo rain scenarios. Future releases must add scenarios rather than replace these baselines.
