# Identity Stress Tests

Generate the same person under these difficult conditions:

1. rainy night with neon reflections
2. bright midday beach
3. cold winter environment
4. strong side lighting
5. mild profile view
6. smiling while walking
7. formal makeup
8. wind affecting hair
9. crowded environment
10. cinematic low-key interior

## Pass conditions

- identity remains unmistakable
- no age or ethnicity-presentation drift
- facial geometry remains stable
- scene complexity does not replace the person
- at least 9 of 10 scenarios pass without recovery
