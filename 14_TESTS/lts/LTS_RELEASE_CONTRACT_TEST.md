# LTS Release Contract Test

## Assertions

1. LTS0–LTS9 stages exist and are ordered.
2. LTS maintenance classifications are explicit.
3. Breaking changes require a major version and migration guidance.
4. Empty LTS registry resolves to `not-evaluated`.
5. Independent review is distinct from authorship and authorization.
6. Canonical release documents must agree on version and sprint.
7. Critical contract inconsistency blocks acceptance.
8. Adapter tiers cannot change automatically.
9. LTS status cannot imply production, security, empirical, or resilience certification.
10. Release records are append-only and digest-linked.
