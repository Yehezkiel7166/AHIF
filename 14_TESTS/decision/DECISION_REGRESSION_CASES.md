# Decision Regression Cases

## Case 1 — Kyoto autumn morning

Expected:
- practical layered styling
- walking-compatible footwear
- calm body language
- environmental portrait
- warm or soft morning light

## Case 2 — Tokyo rainy night

Expected:
- rain response
- wet-ground reflections
- identity-safe face visibility
- restrained neon
- realistic umbrella or rainwear logic

## Case 3 — Bali tropical midday

Expected:
- breathable clothing
- sun-aware accessories
- no heavy winter layers
- heat-compatible makeup
- bright but controlled light

## Case 4 — Paris formal hotel evening

Expected:
- elevated but restrained styling
- composed posture
- indoor practical lighting
- portrait-led camera treatment
