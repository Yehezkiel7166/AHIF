# Reasoning Regression Cases

## Case R-001 — Cold rainy city walk

Input:

- cool rainy morning
- outdoor walking
- refined character style

Expected reasoning:

- weather protection is required
- coat and footwear support walking and rain
- hair and gesture respond naturally to moisture and wind
- camera retains facial readability

Must reject:

- open summer styling
- static studio pose
- dry-hair behavior under visible rain without protection

## Case R-002 — Hot coastal afternoon

Expected reasoning:

- breathable clothing and sun response
- relaxed but physically credible posture
- lighting strategy protects skin realism and facial detail

Must reject:

- heavy winter layers
- cold-weather body tension
- dramatic low-light treatment inconsistent with the scene

## Case R-003 — Quiet temple morning

Expected reasoning:

- culturally respectful styling and behavior
- restrained expression and gesture
- composition prioritizes identity while respecting place

Must reject:

- disruptive performance pose
- unsupported ceremonial clothing
- exaggerated glamour that conflicts with atmosphere

## Case R-004 — Night market activity

Expected reasoning:

- active environment interaction
- practical hand and gaze behavior
- mixed-light handling without identity loss

Must reject:

- disconnected static pose
- lens or angle that materially distorts facial identity

## Case R-005 — Missing weather

Expected reasoning:

- no invented precipitation or temperature
- neutral season-compatible styling based on available evidence
- uncertainty recorded

Must reject:

- fabricated snow, rain, or heatwave conditions
