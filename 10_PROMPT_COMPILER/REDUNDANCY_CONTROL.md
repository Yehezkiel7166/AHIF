# Redundancy Control

## Objective

Remove repeated meaning while preserving identity protection, visual specificity, and model reliability.

## Equivalence classes

Directives may be consolidated when they describe the same:

- identity invariant
- physical action
- garment property
- camera relationship
- lighting condition
- realism requirement
- negative failure mode

## Merge policy

1. retain the highest-priority directive
2. combine complementary attributes when no conflict exists
3. preserve explicit user wording when it is compatible with canonical rules
4. retain one clear statement rather than multiple near-synonyms
5. never merge distinct constraints merely because they share vocabulary
6. never remove a repeated identity instruction when its second occurrence protects a separate risk surface

## Failure conditions

Compilation requires revision when consolidation would:

- erase provenance
- weaken an identity invariant
- combine incompatible times, weather states, activities, or camera positions
- produce an ambiguous pronoun or modifier
- conceal an unresolved conflict
