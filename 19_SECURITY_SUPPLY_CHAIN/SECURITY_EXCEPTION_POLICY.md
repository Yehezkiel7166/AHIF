# Security Exception Policy

Exceptions are temporary governance records, never silent waivers.

Required fields:
- exact rule or finding;
- affected assets and versions;
- business justification;
- risk owner role;
- compensating controls;
- start and expiry;
- review interval;
- revocation conditions;
- independent approval.

Expired exceptions automatically become noncompliant. Critical secret exposure and unverifiable executable provenance are not eligible for exception.
