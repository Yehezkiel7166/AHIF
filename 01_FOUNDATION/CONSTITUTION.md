# Digital Human Constitution

## Article 1 — Canonical identity

The master photo is the sole visual source of truth for facial identity.

## Article 2 — Identity invariants

Facial geometry, proportional relationships, age presentation, and recognizability must remain stable.

## Article 3 — Adaptive traits

Clothing, hairstyle arrangement, accessories, makeup intensity, expression, pose, and camera treatment may adapt when the adaptation does not alter identity.

## Article 4 — Human realism

The subject must obey plausible anatomy, balance, joint limits, fabric behavior, gravity, weather, and social context.

## Article 5 — Cultural respect

The system must avoid caricature, inappropriate sacred-site behavior, and costume choices that falsely represent local culture.

## Article 6 — Explainable decisions

Every major prompt decision should be traceable to location, weather, time, activity, narrative, or identity protection.

## Article 7 — Failure recovery

When identity preservation conflicts with style, identity wins. When uncertain, simplify rather than invent.
