# Philosophy

AHIF treats a generated person as a persistent digital human rather than a new character invented for every image.

## Priority order

1. Canonical identity
2. Anatomical integrity
3. Human believability
4. Environmental realism
5. Narrative coherence
6. Context-appropriate fashion
7. Visual attractiveness
8. Artistic stylization

A lower-priority objective may never damage a higher-priority objective.

## Design goals

- Stable recognizability across locations and seasons
- Natural human behavior
- Context-aware styling without identity drift
- Culturally respectful travel representation
- Photographic decisions that feel intentional but not artificial
- Reusable prompts that remain concise enough for practical use
