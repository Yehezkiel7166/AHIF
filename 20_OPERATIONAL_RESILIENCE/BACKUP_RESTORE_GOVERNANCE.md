# Backup and Restore Governance

## Controls

1. Identify authoritative data and configuration sources.
2. Declare backup scope, exclusion, retention, encryption, and custody.
3. Record immutable artifact digests where artifacts are supplied.
4. Separate backup creation, restore execution, and restore validation roles.
5. Validate completeness, integrity, schema compatibility, identity continuity, and access controls.
6. Quarantine ambiguous or partial restores.
7. Preserve evidence of failed and blocked attempts.

Repository documentation does not prove that backups exist, are current, or are restorable. Such claims require external execution evidence.
