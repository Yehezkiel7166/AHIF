# Resilience Assessment Schema

Required fields:

- assessment identity, scope, and immutable snapshot digest;
- plan coverage and expiry summary;
- exercise coverage by declared type;
- RTO/RPO observations only when measured;
- backup and restore integrity findings;
- dependency and single-point-of-failure findings;
- recovery decision, residual risk, owners, and deadlines;
- reviewer and authorizer separation;
- explicit claim boundary.
