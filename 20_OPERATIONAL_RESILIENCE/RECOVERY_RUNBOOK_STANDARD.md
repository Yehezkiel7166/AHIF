# Recovery Runbook Standard

Each runbook must include:

- stable identifier and version;
- scope and prerequisites;
- dependency order;
- role assignments and approvals;
- exact commands or actions where applicable;
- expected observations;
- stop conditions;
- rollback or abort procedure;
- data-integrity checks;
- identity and semantic continuity checks;
- communications checkpoints;
- residual-risk statement;
- last review and expiry date.

Runbooks must be deterministic, least-privilege, reversible where possible, and safe to rehearse in an isolated environment. Placeholders are not executable instructions.
