# Prompt Compiler Scorecard

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Identity lock integrity | 25 |
| Input traceability | 15 |
| Section completeness and order | 10 |
| Contradiction freedom | 15 |
| Semantic consolidation | 10 |
| Human and environmental coherence | 10 |
| Photography and lighting coherence | 10 |
| Serialization and metadata integrity | 5 |

## Thresholds

- `90–100`: pass
- `75–89`: revise
- below `75`: fail

Identity lock integrity below full compliance always fails regardless of aggregate score.
