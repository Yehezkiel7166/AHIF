# Final Validation

Ask:
- Is this unmistakably the same person?
- Does the face preserve the master photo?
- Are hands, limbs, posture, and balance plausible?
- Does styling fit weather, place, activity, and culture?
- Do light and shadows agree?
- Does the subject belong in the environment?
- Is the story understandable?
- Is any detail included only because it looks decorative?

If identity is uncertain, regenerate with simpler styling and clearer facial visibility.
