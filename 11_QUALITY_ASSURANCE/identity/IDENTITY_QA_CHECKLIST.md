# Identity QA Checklist

- [ ] The output is unmistakably the same person.
- [ ] Face silhouette matches the canonical reference.
- [ ] Eye spacing and shape remain stable.
- [ ] Nose and mouth proportions remain stable.
- [ ] Jaw and chin remain consistent.
- [ ] Apparent age remains stable.
- [ ] Skin presentation is realistic.
- [ ] Makeup does not reconstruct the face.
- [ ] Lighting does not hide defining geometry.
- [ ] Lens perspective does not distort the face.
- [ ] Expression remains within the identity-safe envelope.
- [ ] No generic beauty-model substitution is visible.
