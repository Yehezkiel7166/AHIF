# Decision QA

Before compilation, verify:

- [ ] all explicit user constraints are represented
- [ ] no high-priority rule is violated
- [ ] one dominant activity is selected
- [ ] styling fits climate and activity
- [ ] pose fits the activity
- [ ] camera fits the story
- [ ] lighting fits time and weather
- [ ] identity risk is acceptable
- [ ] cultural assumptions are restrained
- [ ] decision reasons are available
