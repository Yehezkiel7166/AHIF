# LTS Evidence and Claim Policy

## Evidence classes

- **Repository evidence:** committed artifacts, parse results, path and link checks, contract assertions, regression baselines, and source fingerprints.
- **Governance evidence:** supplied identities, approvals, decision events, maintenance records, and exception records.
- **Operational evidence:** external execution, service availability, response performance, deployment, recovery, or production observations.
- **Empirical model evidence:** governed image outputs and linked identity and semantic evaluations.

Evidence classes are not interchangeable. Repository evidence can validate framework consistency but cannot prove operational support or empirical model behavior.

## Claim rules

- Cite exact evidence and its scope.
- Preserve adverse, missing, blocked, and superseded evidence.
- Record unverifiable required facts as `hold` or `not-evaluated`.
- Do not infer maintenance, adoption, uptime, support response, backport execution, deployment, rollback, production readiness, adapter promotion, or empirical certification.
- Do not weaken claim boundaries inherited from earlier AHIF releases.
