# LTS Release and Retirement Policy

## Designation gate

An LTS release may be designated only after:

1. the LTS0–LTS7 record is complete;
2. the full repository validation suite passes on the pinned commit;
3. compatibility with all retained contracts is documented;
4. support scope, exclusions, review cadence, and end conditions are explicit;
5. no unresolved blocker or unverifiable required evidence remains;
6. release metadata matches the validated artifacts;
7. reviewer and authorizer separation is recorded when real actors are supplied.

Repository authorship in this baseline does not fabricate named maintainers, reviewers, approvals, dates, or service commitments.

## Retirement gate

Retirement requires a replacement or explicit no-replacement decision, migration guidance, archival integrity, unresolved-risk disclosure, end-of-support date, and append-only event. Historical records remain accessible and must not be rewritten.

Supersession does not invalidate previous artifacts or claim boundaries. It only changes the governed maintenance state after evidence is recorded.
