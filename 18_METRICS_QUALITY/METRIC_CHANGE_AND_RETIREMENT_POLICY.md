# Metric Change and Retirement Policy

Metric definitions are immutable after publication. A changed formula, population, threshold meaning, or claim boundary requires a new specification version.

A retirement record must include:

- retired metric and version;
- reason and effective boundary;
- replacement metric, when available;
- comparability statement;
- affected dashboards and reports;
- migration and historical-display rules;
- independent approval.

Historical snapshots remain addressable and must not be recalculated under a newer definition without being labeled as a separate restatement.
