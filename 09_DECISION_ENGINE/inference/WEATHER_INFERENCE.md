# Weather Inference

Weather affects:
- outfit layers
- footwear
- hair behavior
- makeup durability
- accessories
- posture
- crowd density
- ground condition
- color contrast
- lighting

## Examples

Light rain:
- umbrella possible
- wet pavement
- softer contrast
- compact posture
- protected bag or outer layer

Cold wind:
- stronger layering
- hair and fabric motion
- slightly braced posture
- warmer accessories
