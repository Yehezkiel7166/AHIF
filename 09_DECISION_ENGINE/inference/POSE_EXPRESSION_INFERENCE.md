# Pose and Expression Inference

Pose derives from:
- movement
- object interaction
- emotional tone
- crowd density
- weather
- camera distance

Expression derives from:
- activity
- narrative moment
- character personality
- identity-safe expression range

Examples:
- calm heritage street: relaxed shoulders, subtle observation
- busy crossing: attentive gaze, compact movement
- scenic viewpoint: open posture, quiet appreciation
