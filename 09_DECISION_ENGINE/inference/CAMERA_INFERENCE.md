# Camera Inference

Select camera treatment from the dominant story.

## Environmental portrait

Use when place recognition matters.
- moderate-wide to normal perspective
- enough depth for location
- subject integrated into scene

## Portrait-led moment

Use when identity and expression dominate.
- moderate portrait perspective
- controlled background separation
- clear face visibility

## Dynamic travel moment

Use when movement matters.
- plausible shutter behavior
- mild imperfection
- avoid face-destroying blur
