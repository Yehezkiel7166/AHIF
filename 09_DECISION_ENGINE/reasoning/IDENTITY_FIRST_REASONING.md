# Identity-First Reasoning

## Governing rule

Every reasoning chain begins with canonical identity constraints. Context may adapt presentation, but it must not redefine the person.

## Mandatory checks

Before accepting a decision, verify that it does not cause:

- facial geometry drift
- age drift
- ethnicity drift
- skin-character drift
- body-proportion reinterpretation
- generic-model substitution
- identity-obscuring composition

## Adaptable attributes

The following may change when context supports the change:

- clothing
- hairstyle arrangement
- makeup intensity within the identity envelope
- accessories
- expression within the expression envelope
- pose and gesture
- activity
- camera and lighting

## Non-adaptable attributes

The following remain governed by the master photo and identity protocols:

- core facial structure
- recognizable feature relationships
- apparent age range
- ethnicity and ancestry cues
- persistent identity signature

## Reasoning rule

When a contextual decision increases identity risk, the engine must either:

1. select a safer alternative, or
2. add compensating camera, lighting, pose, and visibility directives, or
3. block the decision.
