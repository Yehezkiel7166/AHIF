# Cross-Domain Coherence

## Objective

A scene is valid only when decisions across domains reinforce one another.

## Required coherence checks

### Weather ↔ Fashion

Clothing, footwear, hair behavior, and accessories must respond plausibly to temperature, precipitation, wind, and season.

### Activity ↔ Body Language

Pose, hand use, gaze, balance, and expression must support the stated activity.

### Environment ↔ Interaction

The subject must interact with surfaces, objects, crowds, transport, and weather in physically plausible ways.

### Identity ↔ Photography

Lens, distance, angle, lighting, and occlusion must preserve identity readability.

### Atmosphere ↔ Expression

Expression intensity and body tension must fit the requested atmosphere and character continuity.

### Story ↔ Composition

Framing and visual hierarchy must communicate the selected activity and environment without turning the identity into a secondary subject.

## Conflict outcome

Any incoherence must produce one of:

- corrected decision
- compensating directive
- explicit uncertainty
- blocking QA flag
