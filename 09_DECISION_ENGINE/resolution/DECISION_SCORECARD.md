# Decision Scorecard

Score candidate choices from 0 to 5.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Identity safety | 5 |
| Physical realism | 5 |
| Cultural fit | 5 |
| Environmental fit | 4 |
| Activity practicality | 4 |
| Story coherence | 4 |
| Character continuity | 3 |
| Visual quality | 2 |

Choose the candidate with the strongest weighted result, not the most decorative option.
