# Missing Input Policy

When a field is missing:

1. infer only what is necessary
2. prefer common, realistic conditions
3. avoid dramatic assumptions
4. state uncertainty internally
5. choose reversible decisions
6. keep identity risk low

## Conservative defaults

- weather: mild and plausible
- activity: walking, observing, or pausing
- expression: subtle
- styling: practical and refined
- camera: environmental portrait
- story: one simple travel moment
