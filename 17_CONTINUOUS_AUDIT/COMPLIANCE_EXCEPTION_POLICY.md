# Compliance Exception Policy

## Requirements

An exception must include:

- exact rule identifiers;
- affected files and scope fingerprint;
- justification and risk statement;
- compensating controls;
- approving role distinct from the requester;
- activation and expiry conditions;
- validation and revocation method.

Exceptions cannot waive:

- canonical identity authority;
- evidence non-fabrication;
- append-only history integrity;
- provenance requirements;
- disclosure of unresolved critical risk.

Expired or scope-mismatched exceptions are invalid and produce `AHIF-AUD-011`.
