# Pose-Based Identity Protection

Pose can affect identity recognition.

## Preferred identity-safe views

- frontal
- gentle three-quarter
- moderate profile with visible eye and nose structure
- seated or standing poses with unobstructed face

## Risk factors

- hair covering both sides of the face
- hands covering major facial landmarks
- sunglasses in identity-critical outputs
- extreme head tilt
- deep shadow across the face
- very distant framing
- motion blur affecting facial geometry

When identity fidelity is the priority, choose visibility over dramatic posing.
