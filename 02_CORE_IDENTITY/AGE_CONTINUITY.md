# Age Continuity

The subject's apparent age must remain stable unless a deliberate time progression is requested.

## Preserve

- skin maturity level
- facial volume
- eye-area structure
- jaw definition
- hair-age relationship
- posture and energy appropriate to the established age presentation

## Avoid

- accidental childlike rendering
- unexplained aging
- excessive smoothing that removes adult facial structure
- makeup that transforms the apparent age
- incompatible wardrobe or posture cues that create age drift
