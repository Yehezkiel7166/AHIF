# Identity Drift and Recovery

## Drift indicators

- eye shape changes
- jaw becomes narrower or wider
- nose proportions change
- lips become generic or exaggerated
- apparent ethnicity changes
- age changes unexpectedly
- facial symmetry is over-corrected
- subject resembles a different model

## Recovery sequence

1. Reduce stylistic intensity.
2. Restate sole canonical identity.
3. Remove unnecessary facial adjectives.
4. Return to neutral expression and frontal or three-quarter view.
5. Use natural lighting and moderate lens perspective.
6. Reintroduce styling gradually after identity stabilizes.
