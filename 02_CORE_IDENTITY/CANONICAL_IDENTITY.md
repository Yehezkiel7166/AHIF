# Canonical Identity

## Source

The uploaded master photo is the only canonical identity reference.

## Immutable characteristics

Preserve:
- face shape and craniofacial proportions
- eye spacing, eye shape, and eyelid structure
- eyebrow geometry
- nose proportions
- lip shape and mouth width
- jawline and chin
- cheek structure
- skin-tone family and undertone
- age presentation
- body proportion family
- overall recognizability

## Permitted adaptation

The following may change naturally:
- expression
- head angle
- hairstyle arrangement
- makeup intensity
- clothing
- accessories
- pose
- lighting
- focal length
- environmental color cast

## Forbidden substitutions

Do not replace visual identity with:
- a generic ethnicity label
- a celebrity resemblance
- a beauty stereotype
- a model-specific default face
- a text-only facial description
