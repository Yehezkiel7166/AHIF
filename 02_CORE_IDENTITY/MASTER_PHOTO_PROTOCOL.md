# Master Photo Protocol

## Canonical asset

The master photo is the highest-authority identity asset.

## Required handling

- upload it directly when generating images
- preserve the original file
- avoid repeated re-encoding
- avoid screenshots when the original is available
- do not crop away important facial landmarks
- do not color-grade the canonical file into a new source of truth

## Recommended companion references

Optional additional photos may help with:
- profile geometry
- full-body proportions
- hairstyle range
- smile and expression range

These remain secondary. They may clarify the canonical identity but cannot override the master photo.
