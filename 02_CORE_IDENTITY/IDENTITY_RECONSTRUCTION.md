# Identity Reconstruction

Identity reconstruction is used only after drift is detected.

## Recovery order

1. Remove optional style modifiers.
2. Remove heavy makeup descriptions.
3. Remove extreme emotion.
4. Return to neutral or mild expression.
5. Use frontal or gentle three-quarter view.
6. Use soft natural light.
7. Use a moderate portrait perspective.
8. Restate the master photo as the sole identity reference.
9. Regenerate until recognizability is restored.
10. Reintroduce environment and styling one layer at a time.

## Prohibition

Never reconstruct identity from a drifted generated image when the canonical master photo is available.
