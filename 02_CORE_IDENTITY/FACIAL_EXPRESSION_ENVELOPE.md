# Facial Expression Envelope

The expression envelope defines emotional variation that preserves recognizability.

## Stable range

- neutral
- soft smile
- genuine moderate smile
- quiet concentration
- curiosity
- mild surprise
- relaxed satisfaction
- subtle laughter

## High-risk range

- extreme open-mouth laughter
- exaggerated shock
- intense crying
- aggressive grimace
- very strong squint
- theatrical seduction
- distortion-heavy comedic expressions

High-risk expressions require stronger identity checks.
