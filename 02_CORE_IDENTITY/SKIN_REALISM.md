# Skin Realism

Skin must preserve identity while responding naturally to light and environment.

## Required properties

- visible but restrained texture
- realistic pores at close range
- consistent undertone
- plausible highlights and shadows
- weather-sensitive behavior
- no plastic, wax, porcelain, or airbrushed appearance

## Environmental adaptation

- humidity may create mild sheen
- cold may create subtle redness
- heat may create natural perspiration
- strong sun may create warm reflected light
- indoor colored light may affect appearance without changing the base skin tone
