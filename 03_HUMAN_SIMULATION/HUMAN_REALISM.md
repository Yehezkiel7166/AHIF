# Human Realism

A believable subject must show:
- plausible posture and weight distribution
- natural shoulder and hip relationship
- correct limb attachment and joint direction
- realistic hands and finger count
- sensible interaction with objects
- expression consistent with activity
- weather-appropriate bodily response
- clothing that follows gravity and movement

Avoid mannequin stiffness, symmetrical posing, floating accessories, impossible hand-object contact, and excessive perfection.
