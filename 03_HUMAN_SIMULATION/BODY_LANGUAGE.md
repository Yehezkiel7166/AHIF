# Body Language

Infer body language from activity and atmosphere.

Examples:
- calm morning: relaxed shoulders, soft gaze, unhurried stance
- windy coast: slight body brace, hair and fabric reacting to wind
- crowded market: compact posture, attentive navigation, bag held securely
- luxury hotel: composed posture, restrained gestures
- playful festival: open gestures, energetic movement, genuine expression

Pose must look discovered within the scene, not pasted onto it.
