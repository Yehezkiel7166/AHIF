# Hands and Object Interaction

Hands must have:
- correct anatomy
- plausible grip
- believable pressure
- appropriate finger placement
- consistent object scale

Object interaction should support the story. Use at most one or two meaningful props unless the scene requires more.

Examples:
- coffee cup held at a comfortable angle
- phone viewed naturally rather than displayed unnaturally
- umbrella aligned with rain and wind
- suitcase handle grasped with visible weight
